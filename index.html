Why AetherX Labs

Accuracy matters

Cloud mode uses speech and translation providers, with additional checks on supported paths. Captions can contain mistakes, especially with music, overlapping voices or unclear audio.

Privacy minded

Offline caption audio stays on your phone; model downloads and SDK diagnostics may use the internet. Cloud mode sends short audio clips to our server and processing providers. See our privacy policy for processing and retention details.

Built for Africa and the world

Low-resource languages are first-class, not an afterthought.

Use captions responsibly

Only capture audio you have permission to process and follow the source app's terms. TokTranslate respects Android audio-capture restrictions; it does not unlock protected streams or grant rights to redistribute audio, lyrics or translations. Captions are an aid, not a certified translation or a substitute for professional advice.

Current caption-app testing builds do not show ads. An optional separate private SDK sandbox is being prepared; see the sandbox privacy disclosure. Floating subtitles are caption controls, not advertising placements.
