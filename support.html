TokTranslate FAQ

Which languages are supported?

Cloud accuracy mode supports many languages, including isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Swahili, Shona and Arabic. Offline mode supports a smaller set.

Why are no captions showing over another app?

Turn on "Subtitles over livestreams", allow "Display over other apps", start live captions and choose "Share entire screen" when Android asks. Some apps block audio capture.

What does the "≈" and amber caption mean?

Two translation engines disagreed, so the caption may be less certain. Treat it as approximate.

Is my audio stored?

Our caption server does not save audio or captions. Cloud mode sends short clips to processing providers, whose retention terms also apply. Offline caption audio stays on your phone, but model downloads and SDK diagnostics may use the internet. See our privacy policy for details.

Captions say the server is unavailable.

Check your internet connection. If you previously signed in with Google, stop capture, open Plans and extra minutes, sign in again and retry: older builds can report an expired account session as a server problem. Update when a newer build is available. If the problem continues, contact us with your app version and exact error; do not send passwords, account tokens or private audio.

Can I capture or share any media?

Only capture audio you have permission to process and comply with the source app's terms. Some apps block capture and TokTranslate does not bypass that restriction. You remain responsible for any rights needed to share audio, lyrics or translations. Song mode is experimental and cannot guarantee separate lead and background voices.

How do I delete my account or data?

Follow our TokTranslate account and data deletion instructions. You can request deletion by email without reinstalling. Cancel any Google Play subscription separately before deleting your account.
