TokTranslate FAQ

Which languages are supported?

Cloud accuracy mode supports many languages, including isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Swahili, Shona and Arabic. Offline mode supports a smaller set.

Why are no captions showing over another app?

Turn on "Subtitles over livestreams", allow "Display over other apps", start live captions and choose "Share entire screen" when Android asks. Some apps block audio capture.

What does the "≈" and amber caption mean?

Two translation engines disagreed, so the caption may be less certain. Treat it as approximate.

Is my audio stored?

No. In cloud mode short clips are sent to our caption server only to produce text and are not stored. Turn cloud mode off to keep all audio on your phone.

Captions say the server is unavailable.

Check your internet connection and update to the latest version. If the problem continues, contact us.
