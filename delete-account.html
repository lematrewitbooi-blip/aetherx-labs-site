Delete your TokTranslate account or data

You can request deletion without reinstalling TokTranslate. Free captions do not require an account, so you can also request deletion of installation-linked data.

Request deletion outside the app

Email support@aetherxlabs.co.uk with the subject "TokTranslate account deletion request". Tell us whether you want your account or installation-linked data deleted. For an account request, use the email address of the Google account you used to sign in, and include your TokTranslate account reference if available. For an installation request, include its reference if you have it.

We will explain how to verify ownership before deleting records; an email address alone is not proof of ownership. If you no longer have the app or your reference, tell us so we can help identify the records safely. Never send passwords, Google sign-in tokens, device/account session tokens, private keys or payment-card details. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Delete directly in the app

In builds with account features, open Plans and extra minutes, sign in with your Google account and choose Delete TokTranslate account. Read and confirm the permanent-deletion warning. This removes the account from our live ledger and invalidates sessions on all devices. If the operation fails, the app shows an error rather than claiming success.

What is removed and retained

  • Removed: the hashed account association, account usage, active app sessions and unused top-up balance.
  • Purchases linked to a deleted account cannot be restored, including after creating a new account.
  • Retained: unlinked purchase-token hashes and revoked status to prevent replay. Installation-level and aggregate usage may remain for quota and abuse prevention without the deleted account link. Hosting security logs and support correspondence are handled separately; we will explain any security or legal retention that applies to your request.
  • We do not persist captured audio or caption text on our server. Cloud providers and Google Play handle their own records under their policies.

Cancel subscriptions separately

Account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription, request a refund or delete your Google account. Before deleting, manage and cancel your subscription in Google Play to avoid further charges. Paid plans remain disabled during setup and validation.
