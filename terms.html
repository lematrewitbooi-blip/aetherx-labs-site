TokTranslate service and subscription terms

Last updated: 3 November 2026

AetherX Labs publishes TokTranslate. Contact support@aetherxlabs.co.uk for service, payment or cancellation enquiries.

Service and availability

TokTranslate provides captions and translations for supported Android playback. Audio-capture restrictions, device compatibility, downloaded models, network availability and processing-provider availability can affect the service. Captions may contain errors, particularly with music or overlapping voices; they are not certified translations or professional advice. Process only audio you have permission to use and follow the source app's terms.

Accounts and editions

Supported offline captions do not require a paid subscription. Cloud services require sign-in. Use the same Google account in the app and the billing page. The APKPure Stripe edition and the Google Play edition have separate payment paths; purchases do not transfer between editions. Do not share account credentials or attempt to bypass quotas.

APKPure cloud subscription

The optional subscription costs GBP 4.99 per month including applicable tax and includes 30 processed cloud-caption minutes per monthly billing period. Song Mode counts both processed voice layers against that allowance. Unused minutes do not roll over and no minute top-ups are included. A separate one-time five-minute account cloud trial does not start a subscription or automatically convert into paid access.

Review the amount, currency and recurring-payment details displayed on Stripe before confirming. The subscription renews automatically until cancelled. Paid access starts only after the server confirms the payment; a browser return or receipt alone does not update the app. Refresh the cloud plan in the app after payment and contact support if it does not update.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancel using Manage or cancel subscription in the APKPure Stripe edition or its account-linked billing page. Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing period. Uninstalling, signing out or deleting your TokTranslate account does not cancel a subscription. If you cannot access your account, contact support for cancellation help before another renewal.

For refund requests or problems with a payment, contact support with the payment reference. Do not send card details or passwords. Requests are reviewed individually. These terms do not exclude or limit applicable statutory consumer rights, including any rights to cancellation, remedies or refunds. No waiver of such rights is obtained through this page.

Privacy and account deletion

Read our privacy policy and account-deletion instructions. Payment providers keep their own records under their policies and applicable law.

Other services and changes

Enquiries about other AetherX Labs products are not orders or payments. We will confirm scope and price before requesting payment. Material changes to a paid offer will be disclosed before they apply, with any notices or choices required by law.
