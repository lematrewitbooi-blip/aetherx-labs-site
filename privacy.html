Who we are

AetherX Labs ("we") publishes TokTranslate. Contact: REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_EMAIL.

What the app does with audio

  • Offline mode: speech recognition and translation run on your phone; audio is not uploaded.
  • Cloud accuracy mode: while captions are live, short audio clips from the audio you choose to capture are sent to our caption server, which forwards them to speech and translation providers to produce text. Clips are processed and not stored by us.

Service providers

In cloud mode we use OpenAI, Microsoft Azure (Speech and Translator) and Google Cloud Translation. Their own policies apply to data they process.

Information we handle

  • A random device identifier, used only for usage limits and abuse prevention.
  • Usage counts (minutes processed) for quotas.
  • No account, contacts, location or advertising profile is collected by the app itself.

If you add ads or subscriptions later, update this section for the ad network and Google Play Billing.

Permissions

  • Screen/audio capture: only to caption audio you choose; you start and stop it.
  • Display over other apps: to show subtitles on top of other apps.
  • Notifications: to show that capture is running.

Your choices

You can stop capture at any time, switch off cloud mode, or uninstall the app. To ask about your data, email us.

Children

TokTranslate is not directed at children under 13.
