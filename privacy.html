Privacy Policy – TokTranslate

Last updated:

Who we are

AetherX Labs ("we") publishes TokTranslate. Contact: support@aetherxlabs.co.uk.

What the app does with audio

  • Offline mode: speech recognition and translation run on your phone; audio is not uploaded.
  • Cloud accuracy mode: while captions are live, short audio clips (a few seconds each) from the audio you choose to capture are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our caption server. The server forwards each clip to speech and translation providers to produce captions. We do not save the audio or the captions; they are discarded once the caption is returned.
  • The app only captures audio that other apps allow to be captured. It does not use your microphone for cloud captions.

Service providers

Our caption server is hosted on Render. In cloud mode, audio and text are processed by OpenAI (speech recognition), Microsoft Azure (Speech and Translator) and Google Cloud Translation. These providers may keep data for a limited time under their own terms, for example for abuse monitoring, and their own privacy policies apply. We do not sell your data or use it to train models.

Information we handle

  • A random installation identifier and a signed token created by our server, used only for usage limits and abuse prevention.
  • Usage counts (minutes of audio processed per day) for quotas.
  • Your IP address, which our hosting provider and server see when you connect, is used for security and rate limiting. We do not use it to identify you.
  • The app stores your settings (such as target language) on your device.
  • Free captions do not require an account. Builds with optional Google sign-in send a Google ID token to our server to verify your identity. We do not retain that Google token, your email address or your profile. We store a hashed Google account identifier, an account-session identifier and signed sessions so cloud allowances can follow your account across devices.
  • When paid plans are enabled, Google Play processes payments. Our server verifies purchases and stores purchase-token hashes, plan status, expiry and remaining top-up minutes. We do not receive or store your payment-card details.
  • We do not collect contacts, precise location or an advertising profile in the app.

The app does not show ads. Paid plans are being prepared during testing; purchases remain disabled until setup and validation are complete. Google sign-in and purchase features are only available in builds that include them.

Retention and account deletion

Our server does not persist captured audio or caption text. Usage counters, account links and purchase records are kept to provide allowances, recovery and purchase verification while you use the service. Provider-side processing and retention are governed by the providers' terms. Hosting security logs and support correspondence are separate from the app's balance ledger.

In a build with account features, open Plans and cloud minutes, sign in with Google, and choose Delete TokTranslate account. Deletion removes the account association, account usage, active app sessions and unused top-up balance from our live ledger. It signs out all devices. Purchases linked to the deleted account cannot be restored to that account or a newly created account. We retain unlinked purchase-token hashes and revoked status to prevent purchase replay; installation-level and aggregate usage may remain for quota and abuse prevention, without the deleted account link.

Deleting TokTranslate does not delete your Google account and does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription in Google Play before deleting your TokTranslate account to avoid further charges. Google retains its own payment records under its policies. See account and data deletion requests to request deletion without using the app.

Permissions

  • Screen/audio capture: only to caption audio you choose; you start and stop it.
  • Display over other apps: to show subtitles on top of other apps.
  • Notifications: to show that capture is running.

Your choices

You can stop capture at any time, switch off cloud mode, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not itself delete server records or cancel subscriptions. To request account or installation-data deletion outside the app, follow our deletion instructions. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and will explain any information we must retain for security or legal reasons. Never send us passwords, Google sign-in tokens or private keys.

Children

TokTranslate is not directed at children under 13.
